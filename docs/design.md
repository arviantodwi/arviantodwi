Theme tokens, custom CSS, layout patterns. Source of truth: `src/app/globals.css`.

- Fonts loaded in `layout.tsx` via `next/font/google`, injected as CSS vars, mapped in `@theme`.
- `:root` defines `--background`/`--foreground` (`#040404`/white); dark is the only real theme (`prefers-color-scheme` block is identical defaults). `bg-background` on the page wrapper.
- Tailwind 4: no config file; tokens above are the config.

## Recurring visual conventions

- **Supported viewport width**: 375px minimum. Below-`sm` adaptations cover 375–639px; `sm` starts at 640px.
- **Portfolio heading and anchors**: below `lg`, the title, subtitle, and divider have no additional inner padding (`px-0 lg:px-18` on the heading wrapper). They align with the section gutters: 24px below `md`, 72px at `md`/`lg`, and 120px from `xl`. Indonesian `Portofolio` uses 64px type below `sm`; English stays at 76px. From `sm`, both use 76px, then 84px at `md` and 112px at `xl`. The subtitle stacks below the title under `sm`; from `sm`, it sits to the right with a 24px gap and bottom alignment. Through `md`, the title keeps its width and the subtitle flexes/wraps into the remaining space; from `lg`, the subtitle uses 420px. The `#portfolio` target has scroll margins of 84px (base), 92px (`md`), and 100px (`xl`): the collapsed header height plus 24px clearance.
- **Sticky Portfolio header**: enabled from `sm` (640px), with top offsets matching the collapsed topbar: 60px at `sm`, 68px from `md`, and 76px from `xl`. It shares the desktop 40px top spacing (`--portfolio-title-gap`) and 8px backdrop blur. Below `lg`, the band keeps its content-container width; from `lg`, it retains the viewport-wide band. Existing `PortfolioItemFade` follows the heading's measured position, and the containing block still releases the header before the final two cards.
- **Portfolio covers**: both card variants use a 5:3 aspect ratio below `md`, so their height scales with their width in the single-column layout. Below `sm`, previews retain their edge-to-edge negative margins and square corners. At `sm` only (640–767px), the negative margins reset so covers align with the inner Portfolio container: 592px wide and 355.2px tall inside the centered 640px shell and its 24px gutters. From `sm` upward, covers use `rounded-lg`. At `md`, covers use the desktop artboard ratios: 40:17 for the featured card and 73:45 for compact cards. These scale with the columns rather than keeping desktop heights on narrower screens; at the 1440px shell they still render at 1200×510px and 584×360px. Image `sizes` follows the actual gutters and 32px column gap.
- **Footer contact overlap**: the contact wrapper's `-216px` bottom margin is balanced by 220px top padding on the footer band at every breakpoint, plus its 56px inner padding. The wordmark starts below the contact panel; its width is capped at 435px and the available column width, with automatic height to preserve its proportions.
- **Footer link categories**: below 600px, the brand/copy, In page and Social stack with 28px gaps (`gap-7`) between the groups. From 600px until `lg` (1024px), the link categories share the full section width in two equal columns with a 32px gap, In page on the left and Social on the right; the brand stays 56px above them. At `lg` and above, the brand and link group sit side by side, with the existing 200px link columns and 64px gap.
- **Gold underline headline pattern**: `<span className="underline decoration-1 decoration-gold underline-offset-[5px]">` on highlighted h1/h2 phrase (Hero, TechStack, Portfolio, Testimonial).
- **Section shell**: shared `ui/Section.tsx` — `w-full` fills the available width before applying the breakpoint caps, preventing auto margins from shrinking sections to their content or allowing intrinsic content to widen them. Inline gutters are 24px (`px-6`); only at `sm` (640–767px), all section shells, including contact and footer content, cap at 640px and center (`sm:max-md:mx-auto sm:max-md:max-w-[640px]`). This cap includes the gutters; the TechStack marquee rows break out to the viewport edges. The fixed topbar uses its own wrapper and stays viewport-wide. From `md`, the caps are 672px, then 896px at `lg`, then 1440px with 120px gutters at `xl` (1200px maximum content width). Render with `as="div"` for inner containers. Per-section overrides go through `className` (tailwind-merge resolves tiers — e.g. Portfolio passes `md:max-w-none md:px-18 lg:max-w-none xl:pb-20` for its full-bleed grid).
- **Vertical rhythm**: sections get their spacing from the `page.tsx` Body wrapper (`gap-20 lg:gap-[120px]`, `pb-20 lg:pb-[120px]` before the footer) — per-section `mb-*` is gone. Sections keep their own design pad-y (`py-20` on TechStack/AiWorkflow, `pt-20` Testimonial) so xl totals match the artboard's 120px section gap + 80px inner padding.
- **Below-lg adaptations**: hero `pb-[280px]` (photo bleeds below); availability badge above the hero name (`sm:hidden`, `-mb-4`; variant kept in sync with Header's `Status.UNAVAILABLE`); the topbar's far side shows `SocialNav` · divider · `LangSwitcher` below sm (the badge rejoins from sm, social then hides — one divider serves both layouts, so the desktop artboard arrangement is untouched); the hero photo is a dimmed backdrop (`-z-10 opacity-[0.33]`, hung off `right-[-10%] bottom-[-11%]`, no translate — from 600px only the position flips to the sm placement (`top-0 left-[286px]`, insets autoed) and opacity returns to 1, still `-z` behind the copy; at `md` only (768–1023px), `md:max-lg:-top-6` moves the photo up 24px with the copy's removed top padding, keeping the same photo-to-copy alignment as `sm`; `lg` uses a 480px-wide portrait (about 721px tall) anchored to the right edge inside a 728px minimum-height hero, with no extra hero/stat bottom padding; from `xl`, the in-flow wrapper stays `relative` with its insets reset so `Image fill` remains contained. Its 584:877 aspect ratio and `object-contain` prevent stretching. The `xl` row uses a 32px minimum gap and lets the portrait shrink to fit (530px wide at 1280px); `2xl` restores the artboard's 200px gap and fixed 584×877 photo. The hero name uses 100px type at `lg` to clear the face, restoring 116px from `xl`); hero stats `flex-wrap` + `shrink-0` (the Tools stat is `hidden` below 600px — 2 stats on phones, 3 from 600px); TechStack marquee rows span the viewport below md: `-mx-6` cancels the section gutters below sm, and `sm:max-md:mx-[calc(50%_-_50vw)]` also offsets the centered 640px shell at sm (640–767px); its heading stays capped, and md+ keeps the existing constrained marquee layout; AiWorkflow stages stack below `lg`, then use three columns. The toolkit description stays above its logos through `lg`, restoring the side-by-side 320px description and 60px gap from `xl`. AiWorkflow's inner `Section` drops its inline padding below sm (`px-0 sm:px-6` — 24px gutter total) and its AI toolkit logos form a left-aligned 2×2 grid only below sm (`grid grid-cols-2`); from sm (640px), all four logos share one row (`sm:flex`) in equal, shrinkable, centered cells (`sm:min-w-0 sm:flex-1 sm:justify-center`). Logos render at natural size below sm, shrinking to fit narrower cells (`w-auto max-w-full`); from sm they fill their cell up to 196px wide (`sm:w-full sm:max-w-[196px]`); About pulls up under the hero below `lg` (`max-lg:-mt-[200px]`); at `lg` only, `lg:max-xl:-mt-14` reduces the shared 120px section gap to 64px.
- **Muted body text**: `opacity-75` on regular copy, `opacity-60` for tertiary, gold `text-gold` / `<strong>` for emphasis.
- **Numbers**: `tabular-nums` on `Statistic` count-up.
- **Buttons**: `ui/Button.tsx` — neo-brutalism: sharp corners, `border-2 border-black`, offset solid black shadow that presses in on hover (`transition-all`). `<a>` when `href` passed, `<button>` otherwise. `primary` variant = gold fill + `text-background` dark label; sizes `md` (4px shadow) / `lg` (6px shadow, larger padding + text). Icons placed as trailing children (e.g. `TbArrowUpRight size={18}`).
- **Form fields** (`ui/Input.tsx`, `ui/Textarea.tsx`): reusable underline fields per the .pen "Drawer — Input fields" (`uKTB8`) / "Drawer — Text areas" (`azLph`) state system — idle line `#292929`, focus-empty `white/65`, typed value → gold line, error `#ed5440` with a 13px message row below (`gap-2` wrapper). Native prop passthrough (`type="email|number|password|…"`, `min/max/step`, `autoComplete`, `ref`); `error?: string | null` prop wires the message + `aria-invalid`/`aria-describedby`; `scheme-dark` keeps native pickers dark. Line mapping reads `:placeholder-shown`, so pass a `placeholder` for empty fields. Labels stay caller-owned (sr-only in ContactForm). ContactForm consumes both.
- **Contact interest selector**: below 600px, `ui/Select` displays the current interest with the selected radio's gold border/text, 48px height and 16px inline padding; `IconSelector` replaces the square dot. Its trigger-width popover shares the language switcher's frosted surface, option styling, body portal, fixed/document anchoring, edge flipping, outside-pointer/Escape dismissal and scroll behavior through `ui/Popover`. The select adds combobox/listbox semantics, arrow/Home/End navigation, typeahead, Enter/Space selection and Tab acceptance; Escape cancels navigation, blur closes, and focus stays on the trigger. From 600px to `lg` (1024px), the four radios form a 2×2 grid with 12px gaps; at `lg` they return to the 252px left-column stack. RHF synchronizes both controls, and the mounted checked radio supplies the single `interest` FormData entry. Resizing to hide the select closes its portal.
- **Form validation** (ContactForm.tsx): React Hook Form v7 client rules mirror the server's `validateContact` — limits imported from `lib/contact/validate.ts` (`LIMITS`, `isValidEmail`), trim semantics identical, messages from `dict.contact.errors.*` (no new keys). `mode: 'onTouched'` (first validation on blur, then live re-validation on change), `reValidateMode: 'onChange'`; uncontrolled `register()` on all native fields (interest radios registered with no client rule — server whitelist is the authority); the mobile select reads `useWatch('interest')` and writes `setValue` with dirty/touched tracking. Valid submit dispatches FormData through the existing `useActionState(sendContactMessage)` — server stays authoritative; server field errors merge via `setError(type: 'server')`, success calls `reset()` (RHF-idiomatic, no remount). Error copy carries `{min}`/`{max}` placeholders filled from `LIMITS` at render via `fillLimits` (Intl `NumberFormat` per locale → `1,000` / `1.000`); ranges never appear in error copy — only in the name placeholder hint (`placeholders.nameRange`, composed: `"(2–100 characters)"`). Message limit lives only in `LIMITS` (max 1000).
- **Contact submit button** is a flat gold block (`bg-gold px-[18px] py-3 font-bold`, send-plane icon, flush right in the 70px row) — deliberately NOT the neo-brutal `ui/Button` idiom; the .pen frame shows no border/offset shadow. **3s cancel window**: a valid submit is held 3s before dispatching — an outline `Cancel` trigger (dict `contact.cancel`, `border-foreground/70` matching its text color) sits beside the button, which is **disabled** during the window/pending (cursor `not-allowed`, no hover) while a flat 3px `#9747FF` progress bar (`@keyframes send-progress`, scaleX origin-left, no track) sweeps below it; the text fields go `readOnly` for the window (greyed value text `white/60`, `cursor-progress`), the interest fieldset goes `inert` at `opacity-80` and the mobile select disables/closes its body portal (checked radio still submits), the whole panel uses the `progress` cursor with pointer on Cancel only, and the FormData is **re-read at dispatch time**, so screen and payload can't drift; both trigger and bar unmount when the window elapses and the server action dispatches (hidden inputs `startedAt`/`locale`/honeypot untouched). Cancel restores everything.
- **Autofill styling** (globals.css base): Chrome's `:-webkit-autofill` keeps a very light blue background (forced via inset `box-shadow` so the UA fill can't repaint it), white value text + caret; `aria-invalid` autofill keeps the red `#ed5440` text. Underline line colors (has-value gold / error red / focus white) are border-based and unaffected. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the bar is hidden (`display: none`) — Cancel + the `Sending...` label carry the state.
- **Contact sent state** (pen node `B2C6cA`): on success the whole form subtree (interest radios + divider + fields + send row) animates out (`@keyframes contact-form-exit` — 250ms fade + lift) while keeping its values, then a full-width centered `SentView` (40px gold badge + `TbCheck` in `text-background`, 36px bold heading, 16px `text-white/75` subtext, gap 5, h-372) animates in (`@keyframes contact-sent-enter` — 350ms rise); `reset()` runs when the exit ends so fields never visibly blank mid-animation. `role="status"` on the SentView announces it — the old form-level aria-live region now carries form errors only. Reduced motion: exit hides instantly, enter shows instantly.
- Hero portrait + project images: `next/image` with `fill` + explicit `sizes` prop. Exception: ToolsMarquee uses raw `<img>` (Biome override, see tooling.md). AiWorkflow illustrations/logos: `next/image` with explicit `width`/`height` + `loading="lazy"` — card shells are code (one-sided border colors per stage), inner composition is a flattened PNG (`public/ai-native-*.png`, exact 2× of the 373×132 design card, so `w-full` reproduces the design height at desktop widths).

## Email templates (`lib/mail/templates.ts`)

Hand-rolled HTML strings — no email framework (deliberately lean: 3 small, stable
transactional emails; validated via the throwaway prototype branch
`prototype/branded-emails`). Full document per email: head carries charset/viewport,
`color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` metas, a Plus Jakarta Sans variable
`@font-face` (400–700, gstatic woff2; Gmail strips remote fonts → system fallback
stack) and the scheme `<style>` block; body wraps a fluid full-width table card
(`width:100%` — spans the reader viewport, Outlook Word-engine safe: `width` attr
- `bgcolor` + `td` padding `28px 20px 24px`) with a centered footer note `<p>` below.

* **Scheme**: light values inline everywhere as the base (clients without
  media-query support — Gmail, Outlook desktop — render light); dark overrides in
  `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` via classes `e-page`/`e-card`/`e-text`/
  `e-muted`/`e-rule`/`e-gold`, all `!important` to beat the inline literals.
* **Palette**: dark `#040404` page / `#1a1a1a` card / white text / gold `#e4b600`;
  light `#ffffff` page / `#fafafa` card / `#171717` text / gold `#8f6f00` (darker
  for contrast on white); hairlines `white/15` dark, `black/12` light — mirrors the
  site's card-on-bg pattern.
* **Wordmark**: transparent PNG swap pair hosted at arvian.to —
  `logo-raster-dark.png` (black, shows on light) + `logo-raster-light.png` (white,
  shows on dark), 119×22 display, flipped by the same media query (`logo-on-light`/
  `logo-on-dark` classes). PNG only: WebP breaks Outlook desktop and Gmail flattens
  its transparency to JPG.
* **Copy**: owner notification EN-only regardless of the visitor's locale
  (Reply-To = visitor), subject `New: <name> – <interest>`, footer = reply note
  only; auto-reply en/id, greeting `Hi, {name}` / `Hai, {name}`, a no-reply
  `PS:` after the signature (13px italic muted, gold mailto link to the owner
  inbox — `CONTACT_TO`, passed in as `ownerEmail`), footer = "Arvianto's
  website" note + social links (GitHub · LinkedIn · Download CV) — owner
  email has no socials. Message body renders one `<p>` per content line —
  blank lines collapse (paragraph margins carry the spacing, no `&nbsp;`
  spacers). All user input passes `escapeHtml()`.
